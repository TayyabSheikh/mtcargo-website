A Legacy of Trust & Compliance

Established in 1989, MT-Cargo Division brings over 35 years of unparalleled expertise to the logistics landscape of Pakistan.

We do far more than just move cargo. Our team expertly navigates the intricate regulatory frameworks of duty-free exemptions, grant-in-aid clearances, and cross-border transit. Proudly serving international development agencies, line ministries, and vital infrastructure projects, we maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy for non-compliance to protect our clients' interests at all times.
